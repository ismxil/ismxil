About

I'm Ahmad, a product designer who's spent the past decade deep in fintech and banking, figuring out how to make complex financial products feel simple. I started at NCK Technology, a boutique tech firm where I helped build financial products for banks like Fidelity, Access Bank, and UBA. That foundation shaped how I think about design at scale.

From there, I moved to Gomoney and Sterling Bank as Lead Designer, then went on to work with Lemfi and Accenture in Berlin, helping brands like Deutsche Bank and Motel One improve their digital experience.

I love the space where design meets business — where decisions have real impact and good work actually ships. If you're building something in fintech or need a design partner who understands the space, let's talk.
